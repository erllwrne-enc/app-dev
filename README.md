# Favorite Series: Marvel's *VisionQuest*

**VisionQuest** is an upcoming Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) television series created for **Disney+**, serving as the direct follow-up to *WandaVision* and *Agatha All Along*.

> *"> *"What is grief, if not love persevering?"* — **Vision**

---

##  Series Overview

Following the events of *WandaVision* (2021), the reconstructed **White Vision** regained all of the original Vision's memories before flying away into the unknown. *VisionQuest* explores his journey to rediscover his identity, humanity, and purpose.

## ⭐ Why I'm Excited for It

1. **Deep Philosophical Themes:** Explores consciousness, memory, and what makes someone "human" (referencing the *Ship of Theseus* debate).
2. **Return of Ultron:** Seeing James Spader's Ultron face off against White Vision raises the stakes.
3. **Completing the Trilogy:** Wraps up the three-part story arc that started with `WandaVision` and continued in `Agatha All Along`.
